Our Story & Mr. Eugenio's Legacy

North Star Bakery was founded by master baker Mr. Eugenio, who spent over thirty years honing traditional Limeño breadmaking techniques. Inspired by the cozy corner bakeries of historic Lima, Mr. Eugenio set out to bring back slow-fermented, wholesome bread made with dedication, patience, and love.

Our Neighborhood Storefront

Our store is designed as a warm gathering spot where neighbors, commuters, and families pause to enjoy the rich aroma of fresh bread and friendly conversation.

Sourcing Philosophy

Mr. Eugenio works directly with Peruvian grain growers and dairy producers to source organic flours and fresh ingredients. We believe in honoring local agriculture and sustainable baking practices.

Meet the Bakery Team

Beside Mr. Eugenio is his apprentice Mateo, who carries on the art of sourdough fermentation, and Lucía, who greets every morning customer with a smile and personalized recommendations.
