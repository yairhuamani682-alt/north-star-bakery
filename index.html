Limeño Tradition & Daily Fresh Baking

Welcome to North Star Bakery! Led by master baker Mr. Eugenio, our neighborhood bakery brings the authentic warmth of traditional Lima baking to your table every morning. We craft slow-fermented sourdoughs, classic Peruvian breads, and buttery pastries using time-honored recipes.

What We're Known For

Neighbors and regulars love us for Mr. Eugenio’s iconic Pan French (crusty French rolls), golden sourdough loaves, delicate alfajores with manjar blanco, and the welcoming hospitality that makes everyone feel like family.
